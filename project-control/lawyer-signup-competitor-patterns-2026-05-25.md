## Competitor Patterns To Adopt

1. Make the lawyer path unmistakable above the fold.
   - Required Jus-Tice markers: "For lawyers", "Open profile", "Personal area", "Get leads", "Talk to us".
   - Do not make lawyers hunt through consumer content to understand there is a paid provider product.

2. Keep first signup short, then enrich profile after contact.
   - Minimum first form fields: first name, last name, phone, email, practice area, city, plan interest.
   - Move license number, business ID, invoice address, logo/photo, articles and testimonials into the dashboard/onboarding stage.

3. Sell one clear outcome per section.
   - Visibility: show up where clients search by city/practice.
   - Trust: profile, reviews, credentials and legal content build confidence.
   - Conversion: phone, WhatsApp, appointment and lead follow-up prevent missed demand.
   - Control: dashboard, lead status, notes, support requests and billing support reduce owner calls.

4. Use proof modules on every lawyer-facing sales page.
   - Profile preview.
   - Practice/city indexing.
   - Reviews/testimonials or "proof assets to add".
   - Articles/publications module.
   - Lead inbox and missed-lead reminder.
   - Payment/manual invoice status.

5. Add human fallback next to every high-friction step.
   - Phone/WhatsApp close next to signup.
   - Manual invoice/payment link option until Grow/Meshulam recurring approval is complete.
   - Service-request path for refunds/cancellations/plan changes.

6. Make the personal area feel like a business console, not a static profile.
   - Lead board: new, contacted, consultation booked, won, lost, refund/service issue.
   - Profile completeness: identity, practice areas, city, office address, photo/logo, articles, trust proof.
   - Billing/support: current plan, manual payment status, invoices when available, upgrade/downgrade/cancel/refund request.
