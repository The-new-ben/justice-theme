# Investor Hard Questions Q&A - 2026-05-24

## Honesty Statement

This Q&A sheet is for investor preparation only. It does not authorize public CMS/database edits, demo data creation, public profile publishing, live lead creation, payment charges, invoices, refunds, redirects, canonical/noindex changes, sitemap changes or taxonomy changes.

## Current Best-Practice Basis

- Investor due diligence question guides group hard questions around team, market, product, traction, economics, legal/compliance and risk. Source: https://verve-intelligence.com/blog/startup-due-diligence-questions
- Recent VC-meeting guidance says early investor meetings test traction, moat, unit economics, market, go-to-market and risks. Source: https://www.seedforge.com/blog/what-vcs-actually-ask-in-the-first-3-meetings-and-what-theyre-really-testing
- Q&A prep guidance recommends acknowledging weaknesses briefly, then bridging to mitigation, evidence and the de-risking plan. Source: https://www.platformexecutive.com/knowledge/business-models/exit-strategies/preparing-for-qa-what-to-expect-after-your-business-pitch/

## How To Answer Under Pressure

Use this format:

1. **Acknowledge the question honestly.**
2. **Give the current proof.**
3. **Name the blocker or risk.**
4. **Explain the next de-risking step.**

Do not improvise certainty where the system is not finished.

## Dangerous Answers To Avoid

| Do not say | Safer answer |
|---|---|
| "Everything is fully automated." | "The acquisition, onboarding, dashboard and service lifecycle are working; recurring payment automation is approval-gated." |
| "We can charge now automatically." | "We can route paid intent and manual payment links now; automatic recurring charge needs provider approval." |
| "The demo lead is real" unless true and approved | "This is safe demo data designed to show the workflow without exposing private client facts." |
| "Google will definitely rank us quickly." | "SEO is the long-term engine; we are pairing it with direct lawyer outreach and anti-cannibalization checks." |
| "No compliance risk." | "Compliance is a real risk, so we use approval gates, no fake reviews and no guarantee language." |

## Closing If Q&A Gets Tough

"Those are exactly the right risks. We are separating what is already working from what is approval-gated. The important signal is that the product loop exists: acquire lawyer intent, capture billing context, operate the payment queue, deliver leads, prove ROI, and handle lifecycle requests. The next step is turning that loop into the first paid cohort."
