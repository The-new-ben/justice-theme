## Key Takeaways for Jus-Tice

1. **City pages are a gap** — altit.co.il gets 2 SERP slots from city targeting
2. **Sub-practice depth matters** — alonerez and bayerlaw have granular offense pages
3. **Named lawyer authority** — yairegev.co.il and ese.co.il use real attorneys for E-E-A-T
4. **Brand search is defensive** — dok.co.il (#4) ranks primarily on brand, not content
5. **None are unbeatable** — KD 42 with our 266 referring domains is achievable in 6-9 months
6. **Toxic anchors are our biggest handicap** — Clean competitors have 0 gambling anchors
