- ✅ YouTube video integration (legal explainers)

## 2. kolzchut.org.il (כל זכות) — UNTOUCHABLE

**Type:** Government-Backed Non-Profit Rights Encyclopedia
**Status:** National project since 2021, funded by Ministry of Justice + Israel Digital Agency
**Domain Rating: 78** (among highest in Israeli web)

### Why They're Unbeatable
1. Governmental status → Google treats it like `.gov.il`
2. Natural backlinks from ALL gov.il ministries, municipalities, academic institutions
3. 7,000+ entries — topical authority across ALL legal verticals simultaneously
4. Written in simple plain Hebrew — matches exactly how people search
5. Zero commercial intent — rewards by Google for YMYL non-commercial sources
6. Wikipedia citations and references

### Strategic Implication for jus-tice
- **You CANNOT out-kolzchut kolzchut on informational queries**
- WIN by targeting: transactional queries (עורך דין X), cost queries (כמה עולה X), comparison queries
- KEY GAP: kolzchut ranks "מזונות ילדים" but NOT "עורך דין מזונות" — that's jus-tice territory
- Use kolzchut as link-building target: publish research they don't cover → request link

## 🎯 Strategic Takeaways

### 1. Content Volume is the Battleground
Every successful competitor has deep, extensive content. jus-tice has 1,199 articles — the battle is organizing, optimizing, and expanding them.

### 2. The Calculator Gap is the Biggest Opportunity
- **None of these law firm sites have calculators** (only midrag, as a directory)
- **מחשבון מזונות ילדים** = unique in the law firm space
- Calculator pages earn backlinks naturally + extreme dwell time

### 3. Transactional vs. Informational
- Don't fight kolzchut on informational queries (they have DR 78 + government status)
- DO target: transactional (עורך דין X), cost queries (כמה עולה), comparison queries

### 4. Schema is Non-Negotiable
- lawreviews ranks broadly because every profile has LegalService + Review + Person schema
- Must add: FAQPage on all practice areas, LegalService on pillar pages, Article on all articles

### 5. Programmatic SEO: Practice Area × City Matrix
- lawreviews generates hundreds of pages via location × practice area
- jus-tice needs: /criminal-lawyer-tel-aviv/, /divorce-lawyer-jerusalem/, etc.
- Each page needs UNIQUE content — not just city name swaps

### 6. E-E-A-T Must Be Explicit
- Every competitor in top 3 shows clear attorney credentials
- jus-tice (as directory) needs: "How we verify lawyers", editorial process, team page with real names

### 7. Multilingual = Free Extra Rankings
- flanter ranks in Hebrew, English, Russian — tripling keyword reach
- Add English + Russian versions of top 10 practice area pages

### 8. Priority Content Investments (ROI Order)
1. Calculator tools (מזונות, שכר טרחה, מס רכישה) — unique, shareable, backlink magnets
2. FAQPage schema on all practice areas — fast Rich Snippet wins
3. City × practice area pages — programmatic SEO expansion
4. Deep sub-topic articles (10+ per practice area)
5. Attorney profiles with full schema
