# Backlink program: measured baseline and verified targets

Written 2026-07-25. Every claim here was checked in the owner's real Chrome or
by web search on the day. Where a method is unreliable, that is stated instead
of a number being quoted.

## What was measured, and the limit of the measurement

A `"jus-tice.co.il" -site:jus-tice.co.il` search returns **8 distinct external
hosts**, and most are our own social profiles (x.com, facebook.com) plus two
directory listings.

**Do not read that as a backlink count.** The same query against din.co.il also
returns 8. Google's brand-mention operator deduplicates aggressively and only
surfaces pages where the literal string sits in indexed text, so it cannot
compare link profiles. A real comparison needs Ahrefs or Semrush; the Ahrefs
connector exists in this session but is not authenticated, so no number is
claimed here.

One difference that IS visible and does matter: **din.co.il has a Hebrew
Wikipedia entry** and jus-tice does not. That is a durable authority signal.
It is also not something we may create for ourselves, since Wikipedia treats
self-authored entries as a conflict of interest.

## The asset we already own and are not using

The fee tables. `/divorce-costs-2025/` alone carries **6 tables, 73 rows and 71
price figures** across family-law services, and the country pages carry
verified price data for Greece, Cyprus, Dubai and Portugal.

Compiled price data is the one thing Israeli business and industry media
actually chase, and the evidence that we can win with it is direct:

**The article ranking #1 in Israel for "שכר טרחה עורך דין" is
[nadlancenter.co.il/article/6510](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/6510)
— 2,143 words with zero external citations.** It sources nothing. A publication
ranking first on a money query with no data behind it is a publication that
needs data.

## Verified targets, in the order worth trying

### 1. Industry publications that already rank on our money queries

- **מרכז הנדל"ן** (nadlancenter.co.il). Ranks #1 on "שכר טרחה עורך דין 2026".
  Not a law firm, an industry title. Editorial contacts published on the page:
  `drork@nadlancenter.co.il`, `nimrod@nadlancenter.co.il`. The pitch is the fee
  table as a data contribution, not a guest post.
- The same pattern applies to any trade title covering real estate, insurance
  or employment that publishes a "how much does it cost" piece without sources.

### 2. Psakdin as a distribution path, not just a link

psakdin.co.il is the legal channel for **mako, ynet and כיכר השבת**, and supplies
opinion pieces to **Calcalist**. A piece placed there can syndicate outward.
This route runs through Ben personally as a practising lawyer, not through the
site, which is the honest framing anyway.

### 3. The business press, on the data story

Globes, Calcalist and TheMarker run cost-of-living and cost-of-service stories
continuously. The angle that works is a specific, checkable claim, for example
what a divorce agreement actually costs across the range and which components
move it, backed by our tables and by the official court fee schedule.

### 4. Hebrew Wikipedia, as a source rather than a subject

Our encyclopedia holds 124 legal definitions of 528 to 812 words each. Wikipedia
editors cite sources. We may not add our own links, but content good enough to
be cited eventually is. This is slow and cannot be forced.

## What must not be done

- No paid link schemes and no link exchanges. Both are explicit Google spam
  policy violations and the downside lands on a site that carries a lawyer's
  name.
- No outreach sent by the agent. Every email above is for the owner to send or
  approve. Nothing was contacted.
- No claim in any pitch that firms are in our index or database. Same rule as
  on-site copy, and it matters more in an email to a journalist.

## Honest assessment

This is the single largest untouched lever, and it is also the slowest. The
on-page work in cycles 1 to 12 moves positions in weeks. Links move authority
over months, and the first one is the hardest. The fee table is the strongest
opening we have because it is a real asset, it is already built, and the
incumbent ranking above us has nothing behind its numbers.
