# Agent Rules — Jus-Tice Content Migration

1. Work only inside `justice-theme/project-control/` for project files.
2. Do not create random new root folders.
3. Do not overwrite prior reports; archive superseded files.
4. Do not change live WordPress without owner approval.
5. Do not change URLs without redirect map and owner approval.
6. Do not import/update all 1,200 articles at once.
7. Do not classify by WordPress category alone.
8. Update `content-master/master-content-database.csv` before any content action.
9. Always log changes in `content-master/work-log.md`.
10. Use statuses: VERIFIED, NOT_VERIFIED, BLOCKED, DRAFT, APPROVED, EXECUTED.
11. Public article content must not include developer notes.
12. Every proposed new article must map to a cluster, pillar, internal links and business goal.
13. Every redirect must be one-hop old URL → final URL, no redirect chains.
