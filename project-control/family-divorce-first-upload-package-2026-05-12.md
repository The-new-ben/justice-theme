## Minimum Safe Public Upload Checklist

MUST PASS BEFORE PUBLIC UPLOAD:
1. Owner approves Wave 1A or Wave 1A plus 1B scope.
2. Final Hebrew copy is prepared for every page included in the upload.
3. Titles/H1s are unique and do not cannibalize each other.
4. Related cards and internal links use approved current URLs only.
5. Disclaimers and safe CTAs are visible.
6. Taxonomy assignments use approved existing `practice-areas` terms only.
7. Protected PDF, DOCX, calculator, article and case-law assets stay live.
8. No fake ratings, reviews, badges, "top", "recommended", "trusted" or unsupported verification claims are added.
9. No redirects, noindex, deletion, canonical or sitemap-removal action is included.
10. Post-upload QA checks page status, canonical, title/H1, visible disclaimers, related cards, protected assets and obvious 404s.

GSC API:
- Required before URL migration, redirect, noindex, canonical, deletion or sitemap-removal decisions.
- Helpful before public upload, but not strictly required for preparing no-URL-change drafts.
