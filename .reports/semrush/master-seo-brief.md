## PART 5: THE WINNING CONTENT STRATEGY

### What Kolzchut.org.il Does That We Must Emulate
Kolzchut (כל זכות = "every right") is Israel's authoritative citizen rights portal. Why they rank:
1. **Pure informational intent** — no selling, just rights education
2. **Deep topic clusters** — hundreds of interlinked articles on same topic
3. **Government-trusted source** — linked from gov.il, Knesset
4. **E-E-A-T maximum** — written by legal professionals, cited by courts
5. **Answer-first format** — each article starts with the direct answer

**jus-tice can't out-authority kolzchut on informational queries. Strategy:**
- Target COMMERCIAL queries (עורך דין X מומלץ) where kolzchut doesn't compete
- Win on DIRECTORY queries (השוואת עורכי דין, דירוג לפי חוות דעת)
- Add unique value: real lawyer reviews, price comparison, tools/calculators

### What LawReviews.co.il Does (Our Closest Model)
LawReviews appears #1–4 across 4+ of our target keywords. Their model:
- Title formula: `[Practice area] מומלץ - לפי [X] חוות דעת | LawReviews`
- H1: `עורכי דין [X]ים מומלצים ע"י לקוחות`
- Schema: **LegalService + CollectionPage + ItemList** — perfect for directory pages
- Trust signal: exact review count in title ("603 חוות דעת")
- Transparent rating system

**For jus-tice to beat lawreviews:**
- Add visible review counts to lawyer listings
- Use identical schema (LegalService + CollectionPage + ItemList)
- Add review-based filtering
- Create practice-area directory pages with: `/lawyers/?area=criminal-law` → proper pillar URL
