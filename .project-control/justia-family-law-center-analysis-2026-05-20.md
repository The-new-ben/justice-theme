## What Justia Does Well On The Divorce Law Center

Justia's divorce page is not just a blog article. It is a law center hub.

Key structure:

- clear H1: "Divorce Law Center";
- plain-language legal explanation;
- strong internal links to adjacent topics such as alimony, child custody, child support, property division, annulment and domestic violence;
- sections that answer decision-stage questions, not only definitions;
- "last reviewed" date;
- topic cards for deeper pages;
- FAQ block;
- popular topics block;
- webinar / video content;
- footer resources connecting users to lawyers, law, forms, state law and marketing.

The important lesson: Justia wins by making one page behave like a guided legal library and a routing hub.

## What Not To Copy Blindly

- Do not copy US legal content or doctrines into Israeli law.
- Do not publish legal claims without Israeli source/legal review.
- Do not add fake reviewer or fake "last reviewed" dates.
- Do not use "best lawyer" claims.
- Do not make a large hub that creates duplicate/cannibalizing pages without URL mapping.
