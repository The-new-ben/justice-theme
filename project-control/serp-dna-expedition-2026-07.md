## 1. What the practitioners actually do (researched, with outcomes)

### The SEO heist (Causal vs Exceljet, 2023)
Downloaded the competitor sitemap, generated AI articles per URL,
stole 3.6M visits in months. OUTCOME: after going public, traffic
collapsed 610k to 190k weekly, consistent with a manual action. The
usable half of the tactic: SITEMAP AND OUTLINE MINING of winners is
the fastest map of what Google already rewards in a niche. The fatal
half: mass thin AI publishing plus bragging. We take the mining,
never the mass-generation.

### Koray Tugberk topical authority (0 to 128k clicks in 123 days)
The documented practitioner framework closest to "copying DNA":
reverse-engineer the winner's QUERY NETWORK, build a topical map that
covers every entailed query with one page each, connect them with a
strict contextual hierarchy (pillar to spoke anchors that carry the
query vocabulary), publish in coordinated batches so Google re-crawls
the whole cluster together. This is exactly our cluster system, done
harder: full coverage, no orphan pages, anchors = queries.

### Correlational on-page cloning (Cora, SurferSEO, PageOptimizer)
Practitioners clone the statistical profile of the top 3: word count
band, heading count, exact-match headings, term frequencies, schema
types. Not magic: it removes every on-page reason Google has to
prefer them. We do this manually per family: the serp-anatomy skill
already extracts the profile; the transplant step writes our page to
match or exceed it (more sections, more schema types, same vocabulary).

### What we adopt vs reject
ADOPT: sitemap/outline mining of winners; query-network topical maps;
statistical on-page cloning (headings, schema stack, vocabulary,
format); exact-match anchor mesh; FAQ and Video schema where winners
carry them; media/press sections as EEAT; criteria-based ranking pages.
REJECT (kills the asset): mass AI thin content (Causal outcome),
cloaking, fake reviews, PBNs, CTR bots. Not ethics theater: these are
the documented ways sites in this exact position died.

## 2. Criminal family recon (320k impr/mo): the two winning DNAs

### DNA A: the practitioner entity page (#1, alonerez.co.il)
Only ~1,700 words. Wins on: name-entity H1; 24/7 token in title;
sub-service silo linked from the top (מעצרים, סמים, אלימות במשפחה,
עבירות מין, צבאי, סייבר: each a page); FAQPage + VideoObject + Place
+ Geo schema; מהתקשורת section with TV appearances. Word count is NOT
the factor; entity plus sub-service mesh plus rich schema is.

### DNA B: the ranking directory (page 1, verdicts.co.il)
~3,600 words. Wins as a directory on a practitioner SERP by being an
EDITORIAL RANKING: H1 דירוג עורכי הדין הפליליים המובילים; H2s are the
RATING CRITERIA (איכות הייצוג, בקיאות בדין ובפסיקה, זמינות ונגישות,
אדיבות והקשבה, שביעות רצון מהמחיר, כישורי טיעון); then the ranked
list. Google accepts a directory here when it looks like judgment,
not a listing.
