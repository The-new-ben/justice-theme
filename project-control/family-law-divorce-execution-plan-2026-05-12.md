## Competitor Research Summary

Sources checked:
- Din directory result for `עורך דין גירושין`: shows a lawyer directory format with hundreds of lawyers and recommendation counts.
- Mishpati / Zap Mishpati: combines lawyer finder, legal guides, forums, legal categories and downloadable agreement/template paths.
- Gerushin.co.il: strong single-firm commercial page with experience signals, broad service coverage, forms and internal service links.
- Gertel Law divorce agreement and child-support guides: long-form guide style with tables, costs, mandatory agreement sections, examples and FAQs.
- Kol Zchut and Gov.il: official procedure references for family-court claims, dispute resolution, agreement approval and divorce by consent.

Competitor pattern:
- They do not rely on one generic divorce article.
- They cover process, costs, documents, court/rabbinical paths, children, support, custody, property, agreements and lawyer selection.
- Strong pages include tables, FAQs, step-by-step flow, examples, document lists and clear CTAs.
- Directory competitors connect content directly to lawyer cards and inquiry forms.
- Official/help content wins trust by linking to procedure pages and explaining what happens next.
