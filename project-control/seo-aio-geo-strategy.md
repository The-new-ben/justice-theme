## Reviews / Reputation Layer

Reviews and reputation should support SEO and AI-search visibility only when the data is real, source-disclosed and compliant.

Approved strategic directions:
- Use reviews as trust evidence, not as fake ranking language.
- Build entity clarity around each lawyer: name, office, practice areas, location, articles, Q&A, source links and review sources.
- Use Google rating/count only when source verified.
- Keep public review text privacy-safe.
- Avoid review schema until real visible reviews and legal/SEO approval.
- Use profile completeness and source verification before subjective quality scoring.
- Disclose paid placements.

AI-search relevance:
- A rich lawyer mini-site with sourced facts, connected articles, Q&A and review-source disclosures is more likely to be understood as a trustworthy entity.
- AI summaries must not invent reputation claims.
- Review themes may become FAQ/content-improvement inputs only after moderation and anonymization.

Risks:
- fake reviews can damage trust and violate platform policy.
- unsupported "recommended/top" labels can create legal/ethical exposure.
- AggregateRating misuse can create Google structured-data risk.

## 2026-05-11 Reputation SEO Addendum

Status: ACCEPTED / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Reputation data should strengthen entity SEO only when it is verifiable:
- lawyer identity;
- office and city;
- practice areas;
- source-reviewed public profiles;
- source-disclosed Google review link;
- manually verified or API-synced rating/count only after approval;
- visible first-party reviews only after moderation;
- public articles and Q&A attached to the profile.

Do not use reputation data for:
- fake E-E-A-T claims;
- "best/top/recommended" labels without policy and legal review;
- review schema before visible real reviews;
- AI-generated testimonials;
- hidden paid ranking signals.

AI-search goal:
The mini-site should read as a source-backed legal professional profile, not as a generic ad page. Reviews are one trust signal, but articles, Q&A, source links, disclosures and profile completeness carry equal strategic weight.
