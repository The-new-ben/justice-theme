## Main Findings

### Damages / Tort Lawyer

VERIFIED:
- The public SERP pattern for `עורך דין נזיקין` is strongly commercial and lawyer-service oriented.
- Common competitor patterns include dedicated law-firm service pages, injury/compensation language, client reassurance, consultation CTAs, subtopics for תאונות דרכים, תאונות עבודה, רשלנות רפואית, ביטוח לאומי and תביעות ביטוח.
- Jus-Tice `/tort-lawyer/` appears in the public sample for a damages/tort query, so it is discoverable and should be protected while primary URL strategy is decided.

Recommended planning:
- Keep `/tort-lawyer/` as the current service candidate.
- Do not create or promote `/personal-injury-lawyer/` until owner approves whether it becomes the future clean commercial slug and a redirect/canonical/sitemap/internal-link map exists.
- Expand the eventual primary page with practical sections: when to contact a lawyer, evidence/documents, compensation factors, insurance/Bituach Leumi boundaries, common mistakes, FAQs, related guides and lawyer cards.

### Bodily Injury / Tort Claim Guide

VERIFIED:
- `נזקי גוף` and `תביעת נזיקין` behave as mixed commercial plus informational guide queries.
- Result patterns include firm pages and practical guides that explain claim elements, evidence, medical documents, damages, negotiations and court process.
- These queries should support the broad damages page, not create several thin competing pillars.

Recommended planning:
- Use `נזקי גוף` and `תביעת נזיקין` as sections or supporting guides under the approved damages primary.
- Keep `/outline-of-tort-law/`, `/punitive-damage/`, `/deep-pocket/` and similar concept pages as support/merge-review assets.

### Road Accident / Car Accident Lawyer

VERIFIED:
- `תאונת דרכים` and `עורך דין תאונות דרכים` SERPs are strongly service-oriented but also include guide-style content about פלת"ד, ביטוח חובה, medical evidence, no-fault compensation and insurance handling.
- Some results mix bodily-injury compensation with traffic/criminal defense when the accident involves license risk, indictment or serious injury.
- This confirms the need to separate car-accident injury compensation from traffic/criminal accident-offense intent.

Recommended planning:
- Protect `/car-accident-auto-injury-lawyer/` as the current GSC-visible/current public car-accident candidate.
- Keep `/car-accident-lawyer/` future-only until a full migration map exists.
- Support the car-accident page with `/israel-road-accident-compensation-law/` and `/compulsory-motor-vehicle-insurance/`.
- Add clear boundary links later to traffic-law content only when the user intent is criminal/traffic enforcement rather than injury compensation.

### Work Accident Boundary

VERIFIED:
- `תאונת עבודה` and `עורך דין תאונת עבודה` SERPs are heavily tied to ביטוח לאומי, work-injury recognition, medical committees, employer negligence, insurance claims and sometimes employment-law rights.
- This confirms work accident should not be added blindly under personal injury alone.

Recommended planning:
- Keep `/work-accident-lawyer/` future-only until the personal-injury vs national-insurance vs employment-law boundary is approved.
- Use official/public source checks and GSC checks before deciding whether work accident is a standalone pillar, a support page, or part of national-insurance/employment architecture.

### Broad Compensation Query

VERIFIED:
- `פיצויים` is too broad as a standalone SEO target.
- `פיצויים נזקי גוף` is usable as a damages-page section or support guide, but should not become a separate thin page before GSC evidence and source/legal review.

Recommended planning:
- Use compensation language naturally inside the approved damages/car-accident/work-accident content.
- Avoid stuffing generic `פיצויים` into every title/H1.

## Decision Impact

VERIFIED:
- The SERP supports a strong Israeli damages service page with practical guide depth.
- The SERP also supports a separate car-accident injury page.
- Work accident remains a boundary topic and should wait for owner/legal decision.
- The current link plan is directionally right: support pages should strengthen the approved primary, not compete with it.
