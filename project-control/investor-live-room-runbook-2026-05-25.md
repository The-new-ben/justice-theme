# Investor Live-Room Runbook - 2026-05-25

Purpose: give the owner a timed, non-technical demo sequence for the investor meeting. The goal is to show one money workflow, reach the value moment fast, prove what is real, and leave the investor with a clear next step.

## Current Best-Practice Basis

- Product demos that convert should revolve around the buyer's problem, show one workflow, move the value moment early, and put proof immediately after the value.
- Customer onboarding research in 2026 emphasizes time-to-value as a revenue metric, not a support detail.
- Pre-demo investor checklists emphasize that investor-ready is not the same as production-perfect: the demo flow must load, look controlled, and be explained honestly.

## Ten-Minute Meeting Flow

| Minute | Owner action | Screen | Say this |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:45 | Frame the problem. | Value cue card | "Legal marketplaces usually stop at listings. Jus-Tice connects discovery, lawyer signup, payment bridge, dashboard and lead follow-up." |
| 0:45-2:00 | Show demand surface. | Homepage | "This is where client demand enters; lawyer acquisition is visible from the first screen." |
| 2:00-3:30 | Show lawyer offer. | Lawyer plans | "A lawyer buys visibility, trust assets, lead follow-up and a private operating area, not a static card." |
| 3:30-5:00 | Show signup and checkout bridge. | Registration + checkout | "The paid intent, manual invoice path and billing details stay attached. No automatic charge is claimed." |
| 5:00-6:30 | Show private-area promise. | Lawyer dashboard gate | "After activation, this becomes the workspace for profile, leads, notes and service requests." |
| 6:30-8:00 | Show payment truth. | Payment proof drill | "A real one-time Grow link exists and was emailed. The next proof action is paying it and verifying transaction/receipt." |
| 8:00-9:00 | Disclose blockers. | Go/no-go report | "Recurring debit, automatic invoices and refund execution are provider-gated. We capture the lifecycle operationally today." |
| 9:00-10:00 | Ask for next step. | Post-demo follow-up | "The ask is funding/time to finish provider approval, demo data, first 20 lawyer sales calls and paid proof." |

## Hard No-Claims

- Do not say recurring subscription charges are live.
- Do not say automatic branded invoices are live.
- Do not say refunds are executed automatically.
- Do not say there is already a claimed demo lawyer unless the owner has created one.
- Do not say a medical-malpractice lead is assigned unless the owner has created and assigned it.

## Owner-Only Live Actions

These are not repo tasks. They require owner/provider-approved live execution.

1. Open the Gmail message titled `REAL Grow payment link ready: Jus-Tice investor demo 1 NIS smoke test`.
2. Open the full Grow payment link from that email.
3. If choosing to prove real money during the meeting, pay the 1 NIS link with a real card.
4. Open Grow transactions and confirm the payment.
5. Check Grow/Morning for receipt/invoice evidence.
6. If a refund proof is requested, say: "The system captures refund requests; real refund execution depends on the provider transaction and owner approval."

## Fast Recovery Lines

If a live page is slow:

> "The live route is checked by the morning runner. I will show the saved report while the page loads."

If asked why recurring billing is not live:

> "We tested Grow recurring debit live. The provider blocked authorization, so today the honest live bridge is a real one-time payment link/manual invoice path."

If asked whether this can make money today:

> "Yes, via first-cohort lawyer outreach and manual Grow/Morning payment links. The remaining automation work is provider approval and recurring product mapping."

## Completion Assessment

- Material advance: the investor room now has a timed operator runbook that aligns demo flow, proof, blockers and next ask.
- Completion: investor/demo operating readiness is about 98%; live one-time payment proof becomes stronger once the owner pays the existing link and verifies receipt/invoice.
- Still blocked: claimed demo lawyer, assigned demo lead, paid Grow link verification, automatic invoice proof, refund execution and recurring debit authorization require owner/provider-approved live actions.
- Safety: local planning artifact only; no public CMS/database content, payment, invoice, refund, lawyer record, lead, redirect, canonical/noindex, sitemap, taxonomy, GSC/GA4, email/SMS/WhatsApp send, wp-admin or provider setting changed.
