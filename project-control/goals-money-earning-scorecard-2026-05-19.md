1. **Demand engine:** users find legal content, trust the site, submit qualified requests, and return.
2. **Supply/revenue engine:** lawyers trust the platform, register, pay monthly, receive measurable value, and upgrade.

Traffic alone is not enough. Lawyer subscriptions alone are not enough. The business starts compounding when qualified demand and paid lawyer supply are connected with visible value.
