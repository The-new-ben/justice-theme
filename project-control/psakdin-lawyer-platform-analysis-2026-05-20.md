## Public Product Pattern

PsakDin is not only a lawyer directory. It combines:

- lawyer index;
- case-law database;
- legal magazine;
- legal forms;
- PsakDin Live / video;
- polls;
- legal-service provider directory;
- lawyer forum;
- legal AI/chatbot entry point;
- lawyer subscription/login area;
- public lawyer profiles with articles and media references.

The strategic lesson for Jus-Tice: competitors build recurring usage through content and tools, not just lawyer cards.

## Public Lawyer Profile Pattern

A sampled public lawyer profile exposed:

- strong H1 with lawyer/firm name;
- breadcrumbs by legal area and city;
- broad practice-area taxonomy;
- video-call CTA;
- long professional narrative;
- external publications/media links;
- linked articles;
- linked legal guides/articles;
- website/social links;
- city/practice-area internal links.

The strategic lesson: a lawyer profile should become an authority hub. It should connect the lawyer to:

- practice areas;
- city coverage;
- articles;
- media mentions;
- review/reputation signals;
- lead/contact actions;
- external proof.

## What Jus-Tice Should Build From This

### 1. Lawyer Authority Hub

Each paid lawyer profile should show verified authority assets:

- professional bio;
- practice areas and cities;
- articles reviewed or authored;
- external links;
- media mentions;
- credentials and Bar/license proof where available;
- review/reputation freshness;
- contact/lead CTA.

### 2. Dashboard Asset Checklist

The private lawyer dashboard should show what assets are missing:

- no professional photo;
- no review source;
- no recent review;
- no articles connected;
- no media/external proof;
- incomplete city/practice-area coverage;
- payment/manual activation not complete.

This makes the subscription feel like an active growth program.

### 3. Content And Media As Paid Value

For Full Service and higher tiers, Jus-Tice should eventually manage:

- profile copy improvements;
- article briefs;
- media/link proof collection;
- monthly authority report;
- lawyer-specific content cluster;
- approved public links from articles to the lawyer profile.

### 4. Legal AI / Tools Layer

PsakDin exposes a legal AI/chatbot entry point and tools/forms. Jus-Tice should compete with practical user tools:

- legal intake wizards;
- document/checklist pages;
- "what should I prepare before calling a lawyer" flows;
- lead qualification before routing to a paid lawyer.

This supports both traffic and lead quality.

## 2026-05-20 Deep Product Addendum

Reference: `project-control/psakdin-legal-library-strategy-2026-05-20.md`.

PsakDin should also be treated as a legal-library competitor, not only a lawyer-profile competitor. Its major moat is the combination of court decisions, magazine analysis, forms, forums, video, providers and lawyer search. Jus-Tice should not copy its text or scrape its database. The safer path is to build from official Israeli sources, create original summaries and connect every legal item to topic hubs, lead intake and verified lawyer authority.

First recommended cluster: Family Law / Divorce.

First safe deliverable: a source map and data model, not a mass import.
