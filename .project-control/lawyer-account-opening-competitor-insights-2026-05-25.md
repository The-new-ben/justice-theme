## Patterns To Adapt

- Sell intent-time visibility, not "membership". Strong directory copy talks about people looking for a lawyer now.
- Sell a complete professional profile, not a thin card. The trust package is image, field, city, services, reviews/proof, answers and clear contact path.
- Sell measurement and ROI. Lawyer buyers need to know what they received, what was contacted and what still needs follow-up.
- Sell account management after signup. The path should continue into dashboard, lead status, service requests, billing and plan changes.
- Sell payment convenience without overpromising automation. Until recurring billing is approved, the honest bridge is a real payment link or manual invoice after approval.

## Translation Into The Site

- Homepage lawyer strip now says the client is searching now and shows four account-opening steps.
- Lawyer plans page now explains why Jus-Tice is more than another index: visibility, trust profile and post-lead management.
- Lawyer registration page now frames the form as opening a business account with dashboard, payment review and value reporting.

## Investor Demo Note

This is a repo-safe public polish pass. It improves the story investors and lawyer prospects see, but it does not create real users, charge cards, issue invoices or assign leads. Those remain controlled operational steps.
