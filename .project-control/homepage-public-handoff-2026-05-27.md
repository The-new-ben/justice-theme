# Homepage Public Handoff - 2026-05-27

Make the public homepage push more visitors from "I am not sure what to do" into a tracked lead path or WhatsApp conversation.

- Added a visible "what happens after the inquiry" handoff block under the customer intake cards.

- Added source attributes for the handoff CTA: `homepage_customer_handoff`, `handoff_cta`, and `public_legal_help`.

## Where To Check

- Primary action: leave a structured inquiry.
