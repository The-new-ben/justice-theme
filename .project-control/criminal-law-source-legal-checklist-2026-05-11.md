## Source Limitations

- Knesset PDFs are useful legal anchors but may be original or amendment documents, not always the cleanest fully current consolidated text.
- Government service pages are useful public navigation anchors but are not a substitute for legal review.
- State Comptroller and court research documents are context sources, not user-specific legal advice.
- Public Defender pages help explain public-representation pathways but do not determine eligibility for a specific visitor.
- Drug/cannabis public-service pages may focus on administrative-fine workflows and should not be generalized to every drug offense.

## Global Legal Copy Rules

Every final criminal-law article must:
- avoid promising outcomes.
- avoid telling a user what to say in an investigation.
- avoid implying guilt, innocence or expected sentence.
- avoid invented statistics, rankings, case outcomes or lawyer achievements.
- avoid fake ratings, fake reviews, fake badges and fake lawyer availability.
- include a legal disclaimer.
- include a last-reviewed date.
- separate general information from legal advice.
- use official/public source links as navigation anchors, not copied text.
