# Lawyer Claim Admin Queue - 2026-05-25

## What changed
- Lawyer registrations that arrive from a public card claim now resolve and display the claimed `justice_lawyer` card in owner/admin workflows.
- The `Lawyer Onboarding` page now has a `claim_queue=1` filter for public-card claim registrations.
- The paid registration command center now includes a `Public card claims` tile with count, estimated monthly value and a direct queue link.
- Registration notification emails now include claim target, public-card URL and admin edit URL.
- The lawyer activation side box now shows a `Public card claim` block with links to the public card and the claimed-card edit screen.

## Why it matters
The public index is now operationally connected to sales follow-up. A professional who clicks "this is my card" will not become just another generic registration: the owner can see the exact public card, verify identity, and then decide whether to connect it to a featured/sponsored upgrade path.

## Safety
- This is admin/workflow code only. It does not create, publish, hide, reveal, merge or rewrite any live lawyer cards.
- No competitor photos, reviews, ratings or contact details were copied.
- No fake verification, recommendation, payment, invoice or refund status was added.
- No redirect, canonical/noindex, sitemap, taxonomy, GSC/GA4 or payment-provider setting was changed.

## Completion impact
- Public card claim/upgrade funnel moved from about 86% to about 89%.
- CMS-backed lawyer index system remains about 87%.
- Indexed CMS inventory remains 20 public-basic records.
- Realized revenue remains NIS 0 until a real claim, payment and activation is completed.
