# Investor Demo Fallback Answers - 2026-05-25

## Purpose

Use this only if something in the live demo is slow, logged out, missing demo data, or challenged by the investor. The goal is not to hide blockers. The goal is to keep control, tell the truth, and point to proof.

## Golden Rule

Say what is live, say what is provider/data gated, then show the report that proves it.

## If The Homepage Is Slow

**Say:** "The live checks passed this morning, but I will not waste the meeting waiting on a slow page. Here is the verified route report and the launchpad order."

**Show:** `project-control/investor-morning-go-no-go-2026-05-25.md`

**Next move:** Open lawyer plans directly: `https://jus-tice.co.il/lawyer-plans/`

## If The Dashboard Requires Login

**Say:** "Correct. The private area is gated. The public gate shows the product promise, and the logged-in dashboard requires a claimed lawyer profile."

**Show:** Dashboard gate, then `project-control/investor-demo-readiness-2026-05-25.md`

**Disclose:** The claimed demo lawyer is one of the two demo-data blockers.

## If The Investor Asks For A Real Payment

**Say:** "I will not fake this. The system captures paid intent and the manual Grow/Morning payment-link bridge is the current revenue path. Automatic recurring billing, branded invoices and refunds need provider-approved proof."

**Show:** `project-control/investor-payment-lifecycle-playbook-2026-05-25.md`

**Disclose:** Real low-amount payment, branded invoice and refund execution are the three provider/payment blockers.

## If Asked "Is This Fully Automated?"

**Say:** "Not the money movement yet. Acquisition, onboarding, checkout fallback, dashboard, lead CRM, follow-up notes and service requests are live. Payment automation is approval-gated."

**Show:** `project-control/investor-payment-overclaim-2026-05-25.md`

## If Asked "Where Is The Revenue?"

**Say:** "Revenue starts with lawyer subscriptions and lead access. The shortest path is manual provider payment links for the first paid lawyers while recurring approval is finalized."

**Show:** Lawyer plans page and the payment lifecycle playbook.

## If Asked "What Is Fake?"

**Say:** "The routes and workflows are live. Demo lawyer or demo lead records, if used, must be disclosed as controlled demo data unless they are actual approved customer records."

**Show:** `project-control/investor-morning-go-no-go-2026-05-25.md`

## If Asked About Refunds / Cancellation / Complaints

**Say:** "The dashboard captures the request and queues it for owner handling. Actual refund execution is provider-gated, but the service lifecycle is represented inside the system."

**Show:** Payment lifecycle playbook and service-request path.

## If Asked Why This Can Win Against Competitors

**Say:** "The competitors have traffic and directory authority. Jus-Tice is building the operating layer around the lead: acquisition, trust profile, payment handoff, dashboard, lead follow-up and lifecycle service."

**Show:** Lawyer plans page and competitor signup funnel audit.

## If The Investor Pushes Hard

**Say:** "That is a fair risk. I am separating proof from blockers. The product loop is visible and checked; the remaining blockers are demo data and provider-approved money movement. That is exactly what the next sprint closes."

## Absolute Do-Not-Say List

- Do not say automatic recurring billing is live.
- Do not say a branded invoice was generated unless the provider generated it.
- Do not say a refund was executed unless the provider executed it.
- Do not say demo data is a real paying customer unless it truly is.
- Do not blame tools or agents. Say what is working and what remains blocked.

## One-Line Close

"This is ready for an honest investor demo: the product and funnel are live, and the remaining risk is concentrated in provider-approved payment proof and controlled demo data."
