## Purpose

Use this when a lawyer asks: "What exactly am I buying?"

This is not a guarantee of leads or outcomes. It is a first-cohort commercial offer for profile visibility, lead intake, dashboard access and follow-up workflow.

## Short Offer

Jus-Tice gives selected lawyers a focused presence inside a legal-search funnel: professional profile, practice/city visibility, lead intake, lead follow-up status and service requests inside a private lawyer area.

For the first cohort, payment can be handled by manual Grow/Morning payment link or invoice while recurring automation is finalized.
