# Investor Post-Demo Follow-Up - 2026-05-25

## Purpose

If the demo goes well, the meeting should not end with "we will talk." It should end with a specific next step, a recap, and a concrete ask.

## Current Best-Practice Basis

- HubSpot recap-email guidance says a recap after a demo keeps the conversation clear and reinforces next steps. Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/recap-email-templates
- HubSpot sales-email guidance emphasizes keeping follow-up concise and making the next step clear and easy. Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/how-to-write-a-sales-email
- Recent investor-follow-up guidance recommends sending promised materials within 24 hours, referencing the specific conversation, summarizing next steps and making a specific ask. Source: https://www.claryti.ai/blog/investor-meeting-follow-up

## What To Capture During The Meeting

| Item | Write down |
|---|---|
| Investor interest | What exactly did they care about: SEO, lawyer revenue, payment, CRM, market, team, or traction? |
| Main objection | What made them hesitate? |
| Promised material | Report, link, recording, payment proof, demo data, model or deck. |
| Decision owner | Who else must see it? |
| Next date | Exact date/time for follow-up, not "soon." |
| Investment ask | Money, operator support, introductions, payment-provider help, lawyer leads, or SEO resources. |

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Timing | Action | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Same day | Send recap with exact next step | Keep momentum while the demo is fresh |
| Next morning | Send missing proof/materials promised | Build trust by doing what was promised |
| 3 business days | Short nudge with one new proof point | Avoid "no decision" drift |
| 7 business days | Ask directly whether to continue, pause or involve another stakeholder | Force a clear next action |
| Monthly if quiet | Investor update: payment proof, paid lawyers, leads, SEO movement | Keep relationship warm without chasing |

## If The Investor Asked For Real Payment Proof

Send only after the owner has explicit approval and real provider proof:

"Following your question about payment proof: we are treating this correctly as a controlled provider action, not a mock. The next proof item is a low-amount real payment link, provider receipt/invoice and, if approved, refund execution. I will send the proof once the provider-side action is complete."
