# Lead Backed Outreach Context - 2026-05-27

Make homepage lead demand easier to turn into paid lawyer coverage by carrying the lead context into the owner-only outreach builder.

## What Changed

- Kept the source lead id inside the admin outreach-builder URL instead of exposing it in the public lawyer registration URL.
- Added a lead-backed revenue context block to `Lawyer Onboarding -> Outreach Links` when the builder is opened from a CRM lead.
- Added a copyable action script for the owner: verify the lead, contact only relevant lawyers, create/update a prospect, then move to manual invoice or payment-link handling only after commercial fit is accepted.
- Prefilled new lawyer prospects with the demand signal from the homepage lead-backed outreach path.
