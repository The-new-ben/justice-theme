## 1. KOL ZCHUT MODEL — THE GOLD STANDARD

Kol Zchut (כל זכות) is the Israeli government-backed rights wiki. It is the single best content architecture model for our Family Law cluster. Here is what they do that we must replicate:

### Structure (from the actual divorce page I read)
Their divorce portal has **7 major sections**, each with sub-pages:

| Section | Sub-Topics Count | Key Pages |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Divorce Process | 5 | Jewish divorce, Civil marriage divorce, Dispute resolution, Closed hearings, Emergency procedures |
| Property & Finance | 5 | Resource equalization, Sharing presumption, Prenuptial agreement, Pension division, Property transfer |
| Child Support (מזונות) | 12 | Spouse support, Temporary support, Get-refusal support, Enforcement, NII allowance, Tax credits |
| Children & Custody | 10 | Co-parenting, Single-parent families, Child's wishes, Visitation schedules, Paternity suits |
| Domestic Violence | 7 | Protection orders, Shelter info, Contact centers, Parental capability assessments |
| Housing | 5 | Rent assistance during divorce, Public housing for single parents, Property tax discounts |
| Legal Aid | 4 | Free legal aid for family matters, Free legal aid for minors, Court support units, Information workshops |

**Total: ~48 unique sub-topic pages under one "Divorce" portal.**

### What Kol Zchut Does That We Must Copy
1. **Rights-first framing**: Every page answers "What is my right?" not "What is the law?"
2. **Who is eligible**: Clear eligibility section on every page.
3. **How to exercise the right**: Step-by-step action instructions.
4. **Official source links**: Every page links to gov.il, Rabbinical Courts, National Insurance.
5. **Aid organizations**: Lists of NGOs and free legal aid at the bottom.
6. **Disclaimer**: Clear legal disclaimer on every page (critical for YMYL/E-E-A-T).

### Our Advantage Over Kol Zchut
Kol Zchut is informational only. They do NOT:
- Connect users to lawyers
- Show lawyer ratings or reviews
- Offer lead forms or contact CTAs
- Provide cost estimates
- Compare lawyers by city

**This is our monetization gap.** We combine Kol Zchut-quality rights information with a lawyer directory and lead generation system.

## 2. COMPETITOR ANALYSIS — DIN.CO.IL & MISHPATI.CO.IL

### din.co.il (LawGuide)
- **Model**: Portal + Forum + Directory
- **Strength**: Massive article database, user-generated forum content, strong domain authority
- **Weakness**: Dated design, cluttered navigation, thin articles mixed with deep ones
- **Family Law coverage**: Articles + lawyer listings + forums
- **Content format**: Mostly text articles, no checklists/calculators

### mishpati.co.il
- **Model**: Directory + Articles
- **Strength**: Clean lawyer profiles with reviews, city-based filtering
- **Weakness**: Articles are secondary to directory, thinner content depth
- **Family Law coverage**: Lawyer directory with practice-area filtering
- **Content format**: Short articles, heavy on directory pages

### Our Competitive Position
We can beat both by combining:
- **Kol Zchut depth** (rights-based practical content)
- **Mishpati UX** (clean lawyer directory)
- **din.co.il authority** (volume of articles)
- **Plus**: Cost transparency, document checklists, official form links — things none of them do well

## 3. CONTENT QUALITY SCORING MODEL

Based on E-E-A-T research, here is the scoring framework for each article:

### E-E-A-T Score (0-100)
| Factor | Weight | Criteria |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Experience | 15% | Does author have real legal practice experience? |
| Expertise | 25% | Is author a licensed attorney? Is content reviewed by one? |
| Authoritativeness | 20% | Does the site have backlinks from gov.il, courts, bar association? |
| Trustworthiness | 40% | Are sources cited? Is there a disclaimer? Is information current? |

### Content Depth Score (0-100)
| Factor | Weight | Criteria |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Word count | 15% | Under 300 = 0, 300-800 = 40, 800-1500 = 70, 1500+ = 100 |
| Heading structure | 10% | Has H2s and H3s in logical hierarchy? |
| FAQ section | 15% | Does the article answer common questions? |
| Checklist/Steps | 15% | Does the article provide actionable steps? |
| Official sources | 15% | Links to gov.il, courts, NII? |
| Internal links | 10% | Links to related articles and lawyer pages? |
| CTA presence | 10% | Has a "Find a Lawyer" or "Contact" call-to-action? |
| Freshness | 10% | Updated within last 12 months? |
