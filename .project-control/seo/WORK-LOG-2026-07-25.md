## Part 8. Method actually used per cycle

1. Score the opportunity from GSC (impressions × proximity to page one)
2. **Verify the real position in the owner's Chrome.** GSC lies in both
   directions: it understated Greece by 14 places and overstated both USA head
   queries into the top 6 when we were off page one entirely
3. Tear down whoever actually ranks
4. Audit the heading spine before writing a word
5. Fix surgically, only from verified figures, never a rewrite
6. Fix the hierarchy in the same cycle
7. Deploy behind a blocking lint gate with automatic rollback
8. Re-fetch and assert every change rendered
9. Screenshot, then log to `loop-state.json`

Codified in `.claude/skills/seo-money-loop/SKILL.md`.
