## Link Strategy

Primary rule:
- The current planning pillar should link down to all first support lanes.
- Each support lane should link back up to the current planning pillar.
- Support pages should link laterally only when user journey and legal process justify it.
- Thin support pages should not become hubs until expanded.
- Specific case-law pages should not receive broad service anchors.

Anchor rule:
- Anchors should be natural Hebrew anchors in final copy.
- Do not stuff exact-match anchors repeatedly.
- Do not use "best", "recommended", "top" or unverifiable ranking language.
- Do not link to future slugs until migration is approved.

Related-content rule:
- Related cards should use the same relationships as this map.
- Do not show unrelated latest posts.
- Do not show international/foreign-law pages in the Israeli criminal-law support set unless explicitly approved as a boundary.
