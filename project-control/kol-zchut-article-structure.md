# Kol Zchut Style Article Structure

Date: 2026-05-10  
Status: EDITORIAL MODEL - no content rewrites executed

## Goal

Use rights-wiki clarity as inspiration for Jus-Tice legal articles: practical, structured, public-facing and calm. Do not copy text from Kol Zchut or any other site.

## Standard Article Sections

For major legal articles, use this structure unless the search intent clearly needs something else:

1. מה הנושא?
2. למי זה רלוונטי?
3. מה חשוב לדעת עכשיו?
4. מתי כדאי לפנות לעורך דין?
5. מה השלבים?
6. אילו מסמכים כדאי להכין?
7. טעויות נפוצות.
8. שאלות ותשובות.
9. מאמרים קשורים.
10. עורכי דין רלוונטיים.
11. מקורות וקישורים רשמיים.
12. הסתייגות משפטית.

## Public Writing Rules

The public article body must not contain:
- NOT VERIFIED labels.
- project notes.
- owner instructions.
- source-audit comments.
- implementation tasks.
- CMS/GSC/CRM notes.
- publication blockers.

Internal notes belong in `project-control/` or private/draft internal WordPress notes only.

## Source Rules

Use sources as support, not filler:
- gov.il and official public bodies.
- court system pages or forms.
- legislation pages where appropriate.
- Kol Zchut-style public resources.
- public legal-aid or rights information.

Do not publish unsupported legal claims. If a claim needs review, either remove it from public content or write it cautiously and keep the review note internal.

## SEO And Design Requirements

Every major article should include:
- clear H1 and intro.
- practical checklist.
- FAQ block.
- internal links to pillar/support pages.
- lead CTA.
- related lawyer section where profiles are approved.
- official/public sources section.
- Article and Breadcrumb structured data where appropriate.
- mobile-friendly headings, lists and CTAs.
