# Lawyer Registration Claim Context - 2026-05-25

## What changed
- The lawyer registration page now recognizes `claim_profile_id` and `claim_profile` from the public card claim/upgrade CTA.
- When the claim target is a public-approved `justice_lawyer` profile, the registration page shows a clear claim context panel before the business account path.
- The form now carries the selected profile id and slug through submission, so the created draft/onboarding record keeps the original public-card attribution.
- The form pre-fills the lawyer name and firm name from the public card when those fields are not already supplied in the URL.

## Why it matters
This closes the next conversion gap after the public cards. The path is now:

1. Public basic CMS card.
2. Owner-facing claim/upgrade prompt.
3. Contextual registration page that names the selected card.
4. Submission record with claim attribution for follow-up and upgrade handling.

That is closer to the competitor directory pattern: profiles are not passive listings; they are claimable commercial assets.

## Research basis used this cycle
- LawReviews frames profile value around verified reviews, profile depth, SEO strength and automated review links.
- Din's registration page frames the lawyer offer around free joining, exposure, targeted inquiries and a fast form.
- PsakDin public profiles were checked as an example of profile-page depth and professional positioning.
- The implementation is original and does not copy competitor text, photos, reviews, ratings or contact details.

## Safety
- No new CMS lawyer/professional records were created in this cycle.
- No competitor photos, reviews, ratings or contact details were copied.
- No fake verification, recommendation, WhatsApp, phone, invoice, refund or payment status was added.
- No redirect, canonical/noindex, sitemap, taxonomy, GSC/GA4 or provider setting was changed.

## Completion impact
- Public card claim/upgrade funnel moved from about 78% to about 86%.
- Overall CMS-backed lawyer index system remains about 86%.
- Indexed CMS inventory remains 20 public-basic records.
- Realized revenue remains NIS 0 until a real claim, payment and activation is completed.
