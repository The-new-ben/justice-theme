Sources checked:

- psakdin.co.il search result.
- Justia Lawyer Directory and Premium Placements pages.
- Midrag lawyer/review search results.
- Israeli divorce-law firm result for user-anxiety language.

## Competitor Patterns

### din.co.il
- User immediately understands: lawyer directory plus legal information.
- Strong language pattern: `עורך דין לכל תחום ואזור`, legal forums, guides, video, case summaries.
- Anxiety reducer: many forums answered by lawyers, free information, broad coverage.
- CTA logic: ask questions, read articles, contact lawyers.
- Adapt for Jus-Tice: combine search + guidance + lawyer profile, but with cleaner visual hierarchy.

### psakdin.co.il
- User immediately understands: legal news/magazine plus lawyer index.
- Strong language pattern: updates, cases, city/region lawyer pages, index by specialty.
- CTA logic: fill details for professional response.
- Adapt for Jus-Tice: stronger content authority and lawyer matching, but avoid cluttered regional link dumps.

### Justia
- User immediately understands: practice area + location lawyer search.
- Lawyer value language: profiles, contact forms, visibility, traffic statistics, claim/update profile.
- Anxiety reducer: compare lawyers by education, associations, practice areas and online presence.
- Adapt for Jus-Tice: lawyer mini-site value must include profile completeness, signed content, videos, contact form, social links, analytics and content requests.

### Midrag
- User immediately understands: recommendations and ratings from prior customers.
- Strong trust pattern: customer reviews after service, updated ratings, quality checks.
- Adapt for Jus-Tice carefully: reviews must be moderated and truthful; no fake ratings, no unverified badges.

### Israeli Lawyer SEO Pages
- Stronger intent language often speaks directly to pain: cost, children, risk, deadlines, court choice, documents.
- Risk: some pages use aggressive claims. Jus-Tice should be serious, calm and evidence-led.

## Applied To Jus-Tice In This Pass
- Homepage H1 changed to direct user intent: `צריכים עורך דין או הכוונה משפטית? התחילו כאן`.
- Hero subtitle now explains the path: guides, lawyer search, initial questions and smart tools.
- Article archive title changed to `מדריכים משפטיים לפי הבעיה שלכם`.
- Single article pages now include an intent panel:
  - מה הבעיה עכשיו?
  - מתי פונים לעורך דין?
  - איך Jus-Tice עוזר?
- Lawyer archive copy now avoids fake ranking language and emphasizes profile comparison and careful contact.
