## Link Strategy

Primary rule:
- The current traffic-law pillar should link down to all first support lanes.
- Each support page should link back up to the current traffic-law pillar.
- Lateral support links should appear only when the user journey naturally connects the topics.
- Testing/refusal pages should reinforce the drunk-driving support lane, not compete with it as separate broad pillars.
- Speeding/points pages should stay focused on driver-license risk, not professional-license or medical-license issues.
- Marvad pages should stay privacy-aware and medical-advice safe.
- Personal-injury road-accident compensation pages must not be mixed into traffic-defense related links unless a separate boundary section is approved.

Anchor rule:
- Anchors should be natural Hebrew anchors in final copy.
- Do not stuff exact-match anchors repeatedly.
- Do not use "best", "recommended", "top" or unverifiable ranking language.
- Do not link to future slugs until migration is approved.
- Do not link users toward fake/unverified lawyer cards.

Related-content rule:
- Related cards should use the same relationships as this map.
- Do not show unrelated latest posts.
- Do not show will/inheritance pages as related traffic-law content just because GSC had a wrong-page drunk-driving signal.
- Do not show car-accident compensation pages as generic traffic-defense related content.
