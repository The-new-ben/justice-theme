# Competitive Analysis — Israeli Legal Portal Market

## Date: 2026-05-08
## Research Sources: din.co.il, Justia, PsakDin, LawReviews, law.co.il, Midrag

---

## Competitor Breakdown

### din.co.il — Market Leader
- **Title:** `מדריך עורכי דין din.co.il` — keyword "עורכי דין" front-loaded
- **Hero:** Search with practice area + city dropdown filters
- **Sections:** Practice areas cards → Featured lawyers carousel (with photos) → Legal Q&A forum → News → "Ask a Lawyer" CTA
- **Monetization:** Paid profiles, featured placements, forum sponsorship
- **SEO strength:** Deep practice area + city page matrix (e.g., /עורך-דין-גירושין-תל-אביב/)
- **Weakness:** Outdated design, slow load, cluttered UI

### PsakDin — Legal Magazine
- **Title:** `עורך דין בכל תחום, פורום עורכי דין וחדשות משפטיות`
- **Hero:** Search + practice area shortcuts
- **Sections:** Latest news → Lawyer directory → Court rulings → Articles → Newsletter
- **Monetization:** Display ads, sponsored content, paid profiles
- **SEO strength:** Content volume (1000s of articles, rulings)
- **Weakness:** Ad-heavy, feels like a newspaper not a tool

### Justia (US model — aspiration target)
- **Title:** `Free Legal Information - Justia`
- **Hero:** "Helping You With Legal Needs" + search
- **Sections:** Practice Area grid (20+ areas) → State grid → City grid → USP blocks → Footer mega-menu
- **Monetization:** Premium profiles ($99-$499/mo), premium placements, legal marketing
- **SEO strength:** One page per practice × per state × per city = millions of pages
- **Key insight:** Free resources first, monetize the lawyers not the users

### LawReviews — Review Platform
- **Title:** `עורכי דין מומלצים: דירוג שקוף לפי חוות דעת`
- **Focus:** Reviews-based trust signals
- **Risk for us:** "מומלצים" (recommended) may conflict with Bar rules

## Critical SEO Findings

### 1. Homepage Title Tag = Ranking Factor #1
Every ranked competitor has "עורכי דין" or "עורך דין" in their homepage title.

**Current jus-tice.co.il title:** NOT OPTIMIZED (generic WP title)
**Recommendation:**
```
עורכי דין בישראל | מדריך עורכי דין, מאמרים משפטיים וייעוץ | Jus-Tice
```

### 2. Hero Search = User Intent Match
All top sites have a functional search with:
- Practice area dropdown
- City/location dropdown
- Search button

This matches Google's understanding of "עורך דין" intent — users want to FIND one, not read about the concept.

### 3. Practice Area + City Matrix = Page Volume
din.co.il's ranking power comes from having a page for every combination:
- /עורך-דין-גירושין/ (practice area pillar)
- /עורך-דין-גירושין-תל-אביב/ (practice + city)
- /עורך-דין-גירושין-ירושלים/ (practice + city)

Each page is NOT a thin doorway — it includes lawyer listings, articles, FAQs.

### 4. Content Authority = E-E-A-T
Legal content is YMYL. Google requires:
- Author/reviewer attribution
- Professional credentials
- Dates (published + updated)
- Editorial standards page
- Disclaimers

### 5. Internal Linking Structure
Pillar pages link to supporting articles.
Supporting articles link back to pillar.
Lawyer profiles link to their practice areas.
Practice area pages link to related lawyers.

## What Jus-Tice MUST Do Differently (to WIN)

1. **Modern design** — din.co.il looks like 2012. We look like 2026.
2. **Better UX** — No ad clutter, clean interface, fast load
3. **Structured search** — Practice area + city dropdowns (like din) but better designed
4. **Content quality over volume** — Fewer, deeper pillar pages beat 1000 thin pages
5. **AI-powered intake** — No competitor has AI lead routing
6. **Transparent pricing** — Justia model: free basic profile, paid premium
7. **Mobile-first** — 60%+ of Israeli traffic is mobile
