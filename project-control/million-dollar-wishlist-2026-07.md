   Court corpus + Knesset OData behind the existing proxy; every answer

   Israeli market has this. Harvey's pattern #1.

   Avvo/Google and the strongest card upgrade possible.

   "answers within X hours", case-focus chips, editorial quote,

   Priced into Featured tier. (Card overflow fixed; v2 next.)
