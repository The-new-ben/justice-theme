## Shared Outline Rules

Every approved criminal-law page should include:
1. What the topic is.
2. Who it is relevant to.
3. What users should know before acting.
4. When to contact a lawyer.
5. Process stages.
6. Documents and evidence to prepare.
7. Common mistakes.
8. FAQ.
9. Related articles.
10. Relevant lawyer/directory block only if real and approved.
11. Official/public source links after source review.
12. Legal disclaimer and last-reviewed date.

Design/UX requirements:
- Mobile-first section hierarchy.
- Clear breadcrumb path.
- Clear lead CTA without fake urgency.
- Related articles must be semantic, not latest/random.
- Lawyer cards must not show fake lawyers, fake ratings, fake badges or placeholder phone numbers.
- Long sections can use accordions only if the content remains in HTML.
