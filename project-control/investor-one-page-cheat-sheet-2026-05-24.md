# Investor One-Page Cheat Sheet - 2026-05-24

## Honesty Statement

This is a meeting-side cheat sheet. It does not authorize public CMS/database edits, demo data creation, public profile publishing, live lead creation, payment charges, invoices, refunds, redirects, canonical/noindex changes, sitemap changes or taxonomy changes.

## The One-Sentence Story

Jus-Tice is turning SEO legal demand into a lawyer subscription and lead-retention platform: users find legal help, lawyers pay for visibility/leads, and the private area proves value through lead follow-up, profile services and support lifecycle handling.

## The Investor Demo Goal

Do not prove every feature. Prove one money workflow:

1. Lawyer discovers the platform.
2. Lawyer selects a paid plan.
3. Lawyer enters billing/onboarding.
4. Owner sees the next money action.
5. Lawyer sees a private dashboard.
6. Lawyer receives and updates leads.
7. Lawyer can ask to cancel, refund, downgrade, upgrade or complain.
8. Owner sees the support/payment risk before churn happens.

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Claimed demo lawyer profile | So `/lawyer-dashboard/` shows the real private area |

## What Is Blocked Externally

| Blocker | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| Real recurring subscription charge | Grow/Meshulam approval and WooCommerce product/gateway mapping are still required |
| Automatic branded invoice | Requires approved Grow/Morning setup and one controlled transaction |
| Real refund execution | Service desk captures refund requests now; execution waits on payment provider approval |

## If The Investor Pushes Hard On Payment

Say this:

"We are not going to fake automatic payments. The live product already captures paid intent, billing details, payment-link readiness, private-area retention and service requests. Recurring billing, automatic invoices and refunds are approval/mapping work after Grow/Meshulam approval."

## The Ask

Ask for capital and operator support to compress the next 30-45 days:

- close payment approval and recurring subscription mapping;
- create 20-30 paid-lawyer onboarding conversations;
- prepare the first repeatable lead packages in medical malpractice, real estate and family law;
- finish demo-grade CRM evidence with real-but-permissioned customer data;
- scale SEO clusters only after anti-cannibalization checks pass.

## Best Closing Line

"The core risk was whether this could become more than a legal content site. The product now shows the operating loop: SEO demand, lawyer subscription intent, owner payment ops, private-area retention and lead ROI. The remaining payment layer is approval and mapping work, not the business thesis."
