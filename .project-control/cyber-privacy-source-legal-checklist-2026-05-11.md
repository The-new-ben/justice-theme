# Cyber / Privacy Source And Legal Checklist - 2026-05-11

Status: VERIFIED / REVIEW ONLY / NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW

Purpose:
Create a source and legal-boundary gate before any public rewrite, merge, title/H1/meta change, internal-link execution, URL migration, redirect, canonical, sitemap or CMS update in the cyber/privacy/data-deletion/online-reputation cluster.

This is not legal advice and does not approve publication. It is a planning checklist for content quality and risk control.

## Why This Checklist Exists

The cyber/privacy cluster mixes several different intents:
- lawyer-service intent: cyber lawyer, privacy lawyer, cybercrime lawyer.
- informational intent: cyber law, cyberattack, data breach, cyber insurance.
- sensitive personal harm intent: privacy invasion, online shaming, defamation, intimate/publication issues.
- criminal-law boundary intent: cyber offenses, police complaints, computer misuse, cybersex trafficking.
- data/right-to-remove intent: personal data removal, Google result removal, police-record/data-deletion boundary.

These should not be merged or rewritten casually. A page about cybercrime is not automatically a privacy page. A page about Google removal is not automatically a legal right to delete every source. A page about defamation is not automatically a cyber page.

5. Defamation / online reputation:
   - Knesset original Defamation Law PDF: `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/5/law/5_lsr_208497.PDF`
   - Knesset Defamation Law amendment PDF: `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/6/law/6_lsr_208498.PDF`

6. Google/platform removal process:

Limitations:
- Knesset PDFs may be original or amendment texts, not always a final current consolidated version.
- Platform help pages explain platform process, not Israeli legal entitlement.
- Every legal claim must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before publication.

## Topic Boundaries

### Cyber Lawyer / Cybercrime Lawyer

Use for:
- unauthorized access, computer misuse, cyber investigation, cyber offense defense, business cyber incident response with legal implications.

Required checks:
- Does the article distinguish criminal defense from civil/business response?
- Does it avoid promising outcomes after a cyber incident?
- Does it link to National Cyber Directorate reporting only as a public reporting pathway, not as a substitute for legal advice?
- Does it clarify emergency/criminal complaint paths where relevant?

Legal review required:
- YES.

### Privacy / Data Breach

Use for:
- privacy invasion, data breach, database obligations, sensitive data, organizational duties, individual rights.

Required checks:
- Does the page cite Privacy Protection Authority sources?
- Does it distinguish individual harm from organizational compliance?
- Does it avoid unsupported claims about compensation?
- Does it explain when evidence, screenshots, correspondence and dates matter?

Legal review required:
- YES.

### Online Defamation / Shaming / Reputation

Use for:
- harmful online publication, alleged defamation, shaming, content removal requests, reputation harm.

Required checks:
- Does it distinguish defamation from privacy infringement?
- Does it avoid telling users to threaten platforms or opposing parties?
- Does it explain evidence preservation before removal attempts?
- Does it avoid guaranteeing Google/platform removal?

Legal review required:
- YES.

### Google / Platform Removal

Use for:
- removing personal information from search results, platform reporting, privacy/legal delisting processes.

Required checks:
- Does it say platform removal is separate from source-page removal?
- Does it distinguish Google result removal from deleting the original content?
- Does it avoid saying every harmful result can be removed?
- Does it cite Google support pages as platform process, not Israeli law?

Legal review required:
- YES for claims about legal entitlement; editorial review for process explanations.

### Police Records / Data Deletion

Use for:
- criminal record/data-deletion boundary, old police record content, privacy-related information correction/removal.

Required checks:
- Does it distinguish police/criminal-record procedure from privacy database rights?
- Does it avoid folding this page into cyber/privacy unless the intent is proven?
- Does it cite the correct official procedure before recommending steps?

Legal review required:
- YES.

## Content Rules

VERIFIED rules:
- Do not invent legal rights, deadlines, compensation numbers or criminal categories.
- Do not describe criminal allegations as proven facts.
- Do not publish examples that expose private facts.
- Do not show fake cases, fake reviews, fake lawyer achievements or fake authority badges.
- Do not copy official/public source text.
- Use official/public sources as references and navigation anchors.
- Add clear disclaimer language for general information and no legal advice.
- For sensitive privacy/defamation/cybercrime topics, encourage preserving evidence and getting legal advice before public escalation.

## Internal Link Rules

- No related-card algorithm change from this checklist alone.
