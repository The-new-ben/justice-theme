## Source notes

### din.co.il

- Direct fetch status: HTTP 200.
- Public title positions it as an Israeli lawyer and law-office index.
- Public description says users can find lawyers across fields and regions, choose a lawyer, send an inquiry, and receive an answer.
- Page contains recommendation, Q&A, practice-area, inquiry, and lawyer signals.

Extracted skill:

- Use a broad city/practice architecture: practice categories, regional discovery, legal guides, Q&A, recommendations, and lawyer listings should reinforce each other.
- Apply to Jus-Tice by preserving city/practice shortcuts in the hero, footer, directory, and thin-page expansion plan.
- Do not expand local/practice pages without anti-cannibalization checks.

### LawReviews

- Homepage frames lawyer choice as a high-stakes decision, then offers targeted search, detailed profiles, and client reviews.
- Homepage profile cards expose specialties, 5.0-style ratings, and review counts.
- About page emphasizes verified reviews, filtering, transparency, invoice/receipt proof for unsolicited reviews, negative-review integrity, SEO/EEAT value, and review collection tools for lawyers.

Extracted skill:

- Treat proof as a product, not decoration: profile completeness, real reviews, review count, verified evidence, and transparent rules are the conversion layer.
- Apply to Jus-Tice by showing review/profile trust only when real. Until verification exists, use safer signals: specialty, city, language, response path, profile completeness, and human follow-up.
- For lawyer subscriptions, sell reputation, profile quality, lead handling, and review-request workflow.

### Lawhive

- Homepage starts with a direct legal-help promise and a service-selection action.
- It reduces anxiety with clear pricing, regular updates, licensed attorneys, and a four-step process.
- About page connects licensed/regulated lawyers, a modern platform, AI efficiency, case sign-in, and a lawyer recruitment path.

Extracted skill:

- Convert uncertainty into a visible process: describe issue, clarify pricing/next step, connect to lawyer, keep user updated.
- Apply to Jus-Tice homepage by making the first action issue/practice/city intake, then immediately explaining what happens after submission.
- Do not promise flat fees, fixed prices, or case updates unless the payment/CRM/dashboard workflow proves it.

### Harvey

- Homepage is professional-side and proof-led: demo CTA, large institutional trust logos, secure professional platform, legal workflow taxonomy, customer stories, ROI/training resources.
- Its strongest pattern is not consumer legal intake; it is credibility for lawyers, firms, legal teams, and complex workflows.

Extracted skill:

- Use Harvey-style proof on lawyer-facing surfaces only: lawyer dashboard, onboarding, subscription plans, profile completeness, lead CRM, payment/invoice status, and review requests.
- Do not put unsupported enterprise AI claims on public legal-help pages.
- For Jus-Tice revenue, translate "workflow credibility" into concrete lawyer benefits: lead status, response history, follow-up reminders, profile health, review requests, and billing status.

## Homepage rebuild rules for Jus-Tice

1. First viewport must answer: what legal issue, which practice, which city, and how to contact now.
2. Public path comes first; lawyer registration/subscription is visible but secondary.
3. Trust proof must be factual: no fake reviews, no fabricated lawyers, no unsupported "verified" claims.
4. Use a short "what happens next" process directly under the hero.
5. Add city/practice discovery without creating duplicate or cannibalizing routes.
6. Keep mobile menu fixed, stable, scroll-locked, and CTA-first.
7. Lawyer-side CTA should sell measurable operational value: leads, profile, reviews, response tracking, CRM, and billing readiness.
8. Preserve approved content and keywords until owner approves a public homepage rewrite.

## Mobile menu skills

- Keep the menu button visually stable after opening.
- Use a fixed overlay/panel with body scroll lock.
- Keep close action visible.
- Put WhatsApp/phone/form/search first for public users.
- Put lawyer subscription/onboarding second.
- Verify at mobile width around 390px before claiming live readiness.
