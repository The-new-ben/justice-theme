# First Paid Lawyer Outreach Sprint - 2026-05-25

## Purpose

This is the fastest non-fake path from investor demo to revenue: contact a narrow cohort of lawyers, sell a manual-payment bridge, and convert the first paid accounts before recurring automation is fully approved.

## Current Best-Practice Basis

- HubSpot describes a sales pipeline as deals organized by stage, from initial contact to closed-won, with clearly defined stages and entry/exit criteria. Source: https://www.hubspot.com/use-case/build-sales-pipeline
- HubSpot sales-sequence guidance recommends planned touchpoints across days, for example email, connection/call, call and follow-up, instead of one-off outreach. Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/sales-sequence
- HubSpot sales automation guidance highlights routing, follow-up sequences, pipeline updates and activity logging as the repetitive sales tasks to systematize first. Source: https://www.hubspot.com/products/sales/what-is-sales-automation

## Sprint Goal

Book 5-8 conversations and close 1-3 manual-payment lawyer subscriptions from a first list of 20-30 lawyers.

## Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Entry rule | Exit rule |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Lawyer fits city/practice and has reachable phone/email/WhatsApp | First outreach sent |
| Contacted | First call/message/email sent | Response received or second touch due |
| Interested | Asked about pricing, leads, visibility or demo | Demo/activation call booked |
| Demo booked | Time agreed for walkthrough | Demo completed |
| Payment link sent | Fit confirmed and manual payment link/invoice sent | Paid or objection captured |
| Closed won | Payment received / invoice approved | Claimed profile and dashboard activation |
| Closed lost / nurture | Not relevant now or no response after cadence | Follow-up date set |

## 7-Day Outreach Cadence

| Day | Touch | Message goal |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Call + WhatsApp/email | Open conversation around lawyer visibility and first cohort |
| 2 | Follow-up WhatsApp/email | Send one proof line: live funnel + dashboard/lead follow-up |
| 4 | Call | Ask directly if they want the first-cohort terms |
| 6 | Email/WhatsApp | Scarcity: limited first practice/city slots |
| 7 | Close or nurture | Payment link, booked demo, or next follow-up date |

## Qualification Questions

1. Which practice areas do you actively want more cases in?
2. Which city/region matters most for you?
3. Are you already paying for marketing or directories?
4. What is one qualified lead worth to you?
5. Would you prefer fixed monthly visibility, lead package, or a hybrid?
6. If the fit is good, can we send a manual payment link/invoice today?
