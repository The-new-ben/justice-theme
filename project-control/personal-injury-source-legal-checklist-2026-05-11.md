Purpose:
Create a source and legal-boundary gate before any public rewrite, merge, title/H1/meta change, internal-link execution, URL migration, redirect, canonical, sitemap or CMS update in the personal-injury, damages, tort-law, car-accident and work-accident cluster.

This is not legal advice and does not approve publication. It is a planning checklist for content quality, user usefulness and migration risk control.

## Why This Checklist Exists

The personal-injury/damages cluster mixes several intents:

- lawyer-service intent: `עורך דין נזיקין`, `עורך דין תאונות דרכים`, future `personal-injury-lawyer`.
- informational intent: tort law, punitive damages, deep-pocket doctrine and compensation-law explainers.
- accident-specific intent: road accident bodily injury, compulsory motor insurance, police accident confirmation, hit-and-run / missing insurance issues.
- work-accident boundary intent: personal injury, employment law, national-insurance benefits and employer/workplace reporting.
- international intent: US personal-injury content that should not pollute Israeli service pages.

These should not be merged or rewritten casually. A tort-law concept page is not automatically a lawyer-service pillar. A car-accident page is not automatically the same as a broad damages page. Work accident may belong to personal injury, employment or national insurance depending on the user intent.

## Official/Public Source Anchors

Use these sources as anchors for future drafting and review:

1. Tort / damages framework:
   - Knesset PDF, Torts Ordinance [New Version]: `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/6/law/6_lsr_vn_525705.pdf`

2. Road accident bodily-injury compensation:
   - Knesset PDF, Road Accident Victims Compensation Law: `https://fs.knesset.gov.il/8/law/8_lsr_209261.PDF`
   - Kol Zchut road-accident rights portal: `https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/נפגעי_תאונות_דרכים`
   - Kol Zchut road-accident bodily-injury compensation procedure: `https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תביעה_לקבלת_פיצויים_בגין_נזקי_גוף_בתאונת_דרכים`

3. Police accident confirmation:
   - Israel Police / gov.il light traffic accident report and police confirmation service: `https://www.gov.il/he/service/traffic-accident-notice`

4. Work accident / National Insurance boundary:
   - National Insurance work-injury benefits portal: `https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Work_Injury/Pages/default.aspx`
   - National Insurance definition of work injury: `https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Work_Injury/pages/מהי%20פגיעה%20בעבודה.aspx`
   - National Insurance injury allowance filing process: `https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Work_Injury/דמי%20פגיעה/Pages/אופן%20הגשת%20תביעה%20לדמי%20פגיעה.aspx`
   - Ministry of Labor work-accident reporting service: `https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-of-a-work-accident`

5. Personal accident boundary:
   - National Insurance accident-injury benefits portal: `https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Accident_Injury/Pages/default.aspx`
   - National Insurance accident-injury eligibility: `https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Accident_Injury/Pages/תנאי%20זכאות.aspx`

Limitations:
- Knesset PDFs may be original or amendment documents, not always a clean final consolidated version.
- Kol Zchut is a useful public information model, but it is not a substitute for legal review.
- Government service pages explain procedures and eligibility at a high level; every legal claim must be checked before publication.

## Topic Boundaries

### Broad Damages / Tort Lawyer

Use for:
- civil tort claims, bodily injury, negligence, damages, compensation, defendant/insurer questions and when a user may need a lawyer.

Required checks:
- Does the article explain the user problem in plain language?
- Does it distinguish tort-law concepts from a service page for finding a lawyer?
- Does it avoid unsupported claims about expected compensation?
- Does it explain evidence preservation without giving case-specific legal advice?

Legal review required:
- YES.

### Car Accident / Road Accident Bodily Injury

Use for:
- bodily injury after a road accident, police confirmation, medical treatment documentation, insurance/compensation pathways and lawyer help.

Required checks:
- Does the article cite road-accident compensation law and police confirmation source anchors?
- Does it avoid mixing property damage and bodily injury without explanation?
- Does it clarify that work-related road accidents may also involve National Insurance?
- Does it explain documents users should prepare without promising results?

Legal review required:
- YES.

Required checks:

Legal review required:
- YES.

### Work Accident Boundary

Required checks:

- YES.
