# Homepage Lead Outreach Bridge - 2026-05-27

## Profit Goal

Move a public homepage lead faster into a measurable lawyer-supplier outreach path without creating live records, sending messages automatically, or claiming revenue before payment evidence exists.

## What Changed

- Added a `Build outreach for this lead` action inside the owner-only homepage situation-card CRM queue.
- The action opens the lawyer outreach-link builder with the lead source, campaign, content id, legal area, city segment, and a short context note already filled in.
- Kept the previous `Create lawyer prospect` action, so the owner can choose between opening supplier coverage first or creating a prospect record.

## Where To Check

- The per-lead button appears only when there is an unworked homepage situation-card lead in the queue.
- Empty queue state still points to the tracked lawyer outreach builder for manual outreach.

## Not Published As Content

- No public page content was changed.
- No CMS posts, redirects, canonicals, noindex, sitemaps, or taxonomies were changed.
- No live lead, lawyer, order, or payment record was created.

## Revenue Readiness

- Before: 54%.
- After this bridge: 55%.
- Remaining blocker: no real homepage lead in this queue and no paid lawyer/customer conversion yet.
- Payment blocker: Grow/Meshulam KYC/payment approval is still not confirmed, so the bridge continues to rely on manual invoice/payment-link handling.

## Honesty Statement

This is not revenue by itself. It removes one manual gap between demand and supplier outreach, but money appears only after a real lead is captured, a lawyer is contacted, the commercial terms are accepted, and payment or invoice evidence exists.
