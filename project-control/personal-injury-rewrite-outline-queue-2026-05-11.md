## Recommended Outline Pattern

For approved Israeli damages and car-accident pages:
1. What the topic is.
2. Who it is relevant to.
3. When to contact a lawyer.
4. What evidence and documents to prepare.
5. Main process steps.
6. Compensation and insurance factors, with legal review.
7. Common mistakes.
8. Related articles and approved support pages.
9. Relevant lawyers or directory path if real and approved.
10. Official/public sources.
11. Legal disclaimer and last-reviewed date.
