Source-backed facts for draft copy:

- Appeal timing is commonly 60 days for medical disability/incapacity appeal after the written decision notice.
- A medical appeals board can uphold, increase or reduce a prior determination, so copy must not imply "appeal is always safe".
- Certain follow-up appeals after an appeals board are only on legal questions in the Regional Labor Court.
- Other claims-officer decisions may have a different Labor Court timing, including 12 months in the cited Bituach Leumi disability source.
- The page should ask users to prepare the written decision, committee protocol/report, medical documents, employment/income evidence, previous submissions and deadlines.
- The page should not give individualized legal advice or promise eligibility, success, faster handling or a specific result.
