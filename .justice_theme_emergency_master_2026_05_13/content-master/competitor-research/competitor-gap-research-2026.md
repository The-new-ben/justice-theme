# Competitor & Content Gap Research — Jus-Tice.co.il
**Generated:** 2026-05-13  
**Status:** Initial research pass based on codex SERP reviews + known site analysis. Browser verification of specific pages is pending.

---

## Strategic Context

Jus-Tice.co.il is positioned to become a serious Israeli legal portal. The business model target is between **din.co.il** (Israeli legal directory + content) and **Justia** (US — deep practice content + verified lawyer directory + lead gen). The site should NOT copy Midrag's "recommended lawyer" model, which carries regulatory risk under Israeli Bar Association rules.

---

## Competitor Analysis

### 1. din.co.il — PRIMARY BENCHMARK

**What they do well:**
- Full practice-area taxonomy with dedicated pillar pages
- Practice + city landing pages (e.g., "עורך דין גירושין תל אביב")
- Paid lawyer directory with real profiles
- Legal guides under each practice area
- Q&A per practice area
- Author attribution on articles
- Mobile-first layout with sticky CTA
- LocalBusiness + LegalService schema

**What they miss:**
- Some content is thin or outdated
- Design is aging in places
- Mobile conversion is functional but not premium

**Opportunity for Jus-Tice:**
- Better E-E-A-T signals (named reviewers, editorial policy page, sources)
- Premium mobile conversion (WhatsApp-first CTA, sticky urgency bar)
- Stronger content depth per article
- More official source citations per article
- Better city+practice page architecture

---

### 2. PsakDin — NOT A DIRECT COMPETITOR

Pure court ruling search engine. No practice-area content, no lawyer directory, no lead gen.

**Opportunity:** Embed or reference real rulings from PsakDin/Takdin as supporting evidence inside Jus-Tice articles. This builds E-E-A-T signals at low cost and makes articles stand out as legally credible.

---

### 3. Justia (US model) — REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

**What they do:**
- Bar number verified lawyer profiles
- Deep practice-area content
- State + city + practice landing pages
- Community Q&A (Justia Answers)
- Comprehensive schema markup
- Named editorial team
- Paid profile tiers with disclosure
- Internal linking: rulings ↔ lawyers ↔ articles ↔ city pages

**What Jus-Tice can borrow:**
- Bar number field on lawyer profiles (Israeli Bar = לשכת עורכי הדין)
- Editorial disclosure page (מדיניות עריכה)
- "Find a lawyer" neutral CTA vs. "recommended lawyer" risky language
- Q&A participation by lawyers
- Tiered paid profiles with clear disclosure

---

### 4. law.co.il — WEAK INCUMBENT

Old design, minimal UX, poor mobile. Mostly legislation text. Low trust signals.

**Opportunity:** Jus-Tice can capture their audience with better UX, fresher content, and a direct lawyer connection CTA.

---

### 5. Takdin / Nevo — DATA SOURCES, NOT COMPETITORS

Both are legal databases for professionals. Use them as official source citations inside articles.

---

### 6. Kol Zchut — HIGH TRUST, NON-COMMERCIAL

Government-adjacent public rights portal. Dominates informational queries about citizen rights.

**Strategy:** Do NOT compete head-on with Kol Zchut on pure informational queries they own. Instead:
- Cite Kol Zchut as a source (E-E-A-T signal)
- Write complementary content that bridges "know your rights" → "find a lawyer"
- Own the transactional/commercial intent that Kol Zchut deliberately avoids

---

### 7. Midrag — RISKY MODEL TO AVOID

Lawyer marketplace with rating system and "recommended lawyer" framing.

**Risk:** Israeli Bar Association rules restrict lawyer advertising. The framing of "recommended" or "best lawyer" can constitute illegal advertising or mislead consumers.

**Jus-Tice approach:**
- Use "find a lawyer" or "request a consultation" — not "recommended"
- Clearly disclose paid placement
- Never present paid profiles as independent editorial recommendations
- Include on every lawyer profile: "זו פרסומת בתשלום / This is paid advertising"
