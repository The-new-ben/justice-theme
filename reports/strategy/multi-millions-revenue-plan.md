## Part 1: What Din.co.il, PsakDin, and LawReviews Actually Do (Deep Scan Results)

### Din.co.il — The Full Picture

Din is NOT just a lawyer directory. Based on my research, it has **three layers of revenue**:

**Layer 1: Lawyer Directory (Core)**
- 18+ practice area categories (criminal, family, real estate, labor, torts, traffic, execution/insolvency, commercial, administrative, IP, civil, tax, immigration, mediation/arbitration, medical malpractice, defense ministry, internet law)
- Each lawyer has: photo, name, star rating, recommender count, location, forum manager badge, view count, description
- Multiple CTAs per card: Zoom, SMS, Call, Consultation
- Revenue: Premium placement fees, featured positions, enhanced profiles

**Layer 2: Supplementary Professionals (The Hidden Gold Mine)**
- Real estate appraisers (שמאי מקרקעין)
- Property appraisers (שמאי רכוש)
- Agricultural appraisers (שמאי חקלאי)
- Actuaries (אקטוארים) — for economic opinions in court
- Forensic psychiatrists — for legal opinions
- Engineers and technologists — for construction defect cases
- **This is a revenue stream you are currently not capturing at all**

**Layer 3: Forum-Driven SEO**
- Lawyers answer public questions for free, which generates massive organic content
- Each answer is a new indexed page with long-tail keywords
- The lawyer gets visibility, Din gets traffic, Google indexes it all

**Traffic (SimilarWeb April 2026):**
- Israel rank: #2,829
- Average visit: 1:16 minutes
- Pages/visit: 1.71
- Bounce rate: 58.8%
- ~90% traffic from Israel

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Din's traffic metrics are actually mediocre (high bounce, low pages/visit). This means their content is NOT deeply engaging — it's just old and has domain authority from age. A modern, premium site with better UX CAN outrank them.

### PsakDin.co.il — The Authority Play

PsakDin is owned by **Yedioth Ahronoth** (one of Israel's biggest media groups). Their model is fundamentally different:

- **Paid rulings database** — you pay to read full court decisions. This is a B2B/B2Professional revenue stream (lawyers and law students pay)
- **Legal forms marketplace** — downloadable legal templates
- **Magazine section** — legal news that drives organic traffic
- **Lawyer directory** — similar to Din but less prominent
- **Strategic partnerships** — with the Israel Bar Association, ynet, Calcalist, N12

> [!IMPORTANT]
> PsakDin has something we cannot easily replicate: a massive media conglomerate behind them. But we CAN replicate their content authority model by publishing high-quality legal articles at scale using AI + human review.

### LawReviews.co.il — The Review + Consultation Play

LawReviews differentiates on three things we should steal:

1. **Verified client reviews** — using "wisdom of the crowd" verification technology
2. **Online consultation filter** — users can specifically filter for lawyers offering remote/video consultations
3. **Multi-language filter** — Hebrew, English, Russian, Arabic, French
4. **Direct scheduling** — book an appointment through the portal
