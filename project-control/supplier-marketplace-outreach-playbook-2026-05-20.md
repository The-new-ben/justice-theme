# Supplier Marketplace Outreach Playbook - 2026-05-20

## Goal

Create a second revenue line around lawyer-facing suppliers.

Jus-Tice should not only sell to lawyers. It should also sell access, visibility and warm introductions to providers that lawyers already need.

Examples:

- Translation, notary and apostille.
- Office rooms and meeting rooms.
- Legal marketing, video, design and SEO.
- Expert witnesses and private investigators.
- Legal tech, CRM, automation and intake tools.
- Accounting, tax, finance and collections.
- Courier, filing and court-operation services.
- Training, conferences and professional education.

## Positioning

For suppliers:

> Jus-Tice is building a lawyer growth platform. We are opening a limited provider network for companies that serve Israeli lawyers and law firms. We do not sell random lists. We collect real lawyer needs from the dashboard and match only relevant, checked providers.

For lawyers:

> Jus-Tice can help you find checked professional services for your practice. We do not send your request to providers before owner review.

## Revenue models

Use these in order:

1. Free pilot / relationship building.
   - Best for the first 5-10 suppliers.
   - Goal: prove lawyers actually request these services.
2. Monthly listing fee.
   - Good for office rooms, translators, legal tech, marketing agencies.
   - Simple target: 249-990 NIS/month depending on visibility.
3. Warm introduction fee.
   - Good where the supplier can close high-value work.
   - Use a fixed fee per qualified request, not vague "lead selling".
4. Category sponsorship.
   - Only after traffic and lawyer dashboard usage exist.
   - Example: "translation partner" or "office room partner".
5. Affiliate/referral.
   - Use only with clear disclosure and written terms.

## Supplier qualification questions

Ask before any public exposure:

- What exact service do you sell to lawyers?
- What cities/areas do you serve?
- What is the typical price range?
- How fast can you respond to a lawyer request?
- Do you have proof, clients, reviews or professional credentials?
- Are you willing to give Jus-Tice lawyers a clear offer?
- Which model fits: monthly listing, warm-introduction fee, category sponsorship or affiliate?

## Compliance and trust rules

- Do not present providers as "recommended" unless there is a real approved recommendation policy.
- Use "checked provider" only after owner review.
- Disclose commercial relationships when provider placement is paid.
- Do not automatically send lawyer details to a supplier.
- Do not create exclusive category promises until supplier quality and response times are proven.
